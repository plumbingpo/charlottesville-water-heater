Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a rush, and a tank with too little recovery runs cold just as the dish room fills up. Charlottesville Water Heater specializes in food-service hot water, including high-input gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, proper sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a core concern for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained kitchen to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, keeping downtime short, and staging temporary hot water whenever the layout allows.

Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Charlottesville Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs anywhere in Charlottesville and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial water heater emergency, including failed gas valves, burned-out heating elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks and fittings, failed recirculation pumps, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.
