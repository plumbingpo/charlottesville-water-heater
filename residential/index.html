Upfront Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Good water heater service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Charlottesville Water Heater provides honest, upfront pricing for every home, covering everything from a burned-out heating element to a full tank replacement. Every visit begins with a thorough assessment of your water heater, including its age, capacity, fuel type, venting, and valve and tank condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

The problem with water heater failures is that many of them start out of sight. Hard-water minerals settle into a crust on the bottom of the tank, the anode rod wears down gradually, and when it's gone, the tank itself starts to corrode. When hot water comes out rusty, the tank rumbles, or a small drip appears at the base, get it looked at while it's still a simple repair.

Not every call is an emergency. Many homeowners call us to evaluate an aging heater before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical tank lifespan of 8 to 12 years (tankless units often run 20 years with annual descaling), replacing on your own schedule beats scrambling for a new heater with water on the floor. We'll give you an honest read on how much life your equipment has left.

Maintenance Plans

Maintenance Plans for Charlottesville Homes

A maintenance visit from Charlottesville Water Heater is more than a quick drain and refill. The technician flushes the sediment, pulls and inspects the anode rod, tests the relief valve, checks the burner, pilot, and venting on gas heaters, checks elements and thermostats on electric models, and confirms a safe water temperature. Minor faults are caught while they're still cheap to fix, and the heater keeps running clean and efficient.

Every maintenance plan we write is a little different. A tank in its later years gets a closer look than a new heater, a tankless unit on hard water needs regular descaling to keep its heat exchanger clear, and a large family cycles the heater far harder than a couple does. We build each visit around your equipment and water, so every service helps keep the unit efficient and protects the equipment you've already paid for.

Think of maintenance as protection for equipment you've already bought. Each visit helps keep the tank clear of sediment, the anode rod doing its job, and the relief valve in working order, which lowers what you pay to heat water while reducing wear on the burner or elements. For most families, maintenance costs far less than replacing the heater ahead of schedule.

Sized for Your Household

The Right Size Water Heater for Your Home

Bigger isn't always better when it comes to water heaters. What matters for a tank is the first-hour rating, the number of gallons of hot water it can deliver in a busy hour, and for a tankless unit it's the GPM it can hold at your incoming water temperature. That's why we never just copy the gallon rating of the old heater. Instead, we look at how many people live in the home, how many bathrooms you have, plus large draws such as a jetted tub or high-capacity washer.

We walk you through the options for every home, especially growing families with rising hot water demands. A 40-gallon tank that served a couple rarely keeps up after two teenagers start showering back to back, and a tankless water heater can deliver steady hot water so long as it's sized for the flow. If you're weighing tank against tankless, or gas against a heat pump model, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: installed price, operating cost, venting and gas line sizing, electrical service, and space, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

The Water Heater Repairs Local Homes Need Most

Homes around Charlottesville run into a familiar set of water heater failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Charlottesville Water Heater handles them all: no hot water, water that only gets lukewarm, pilots that won't stay lit, electric tanks that trip the breaker, rumbling tanks full of sediment, rusty or smelly water, leaking relief valves, and tankless units flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Not every water heater problem is equally urgent. A failed element just means lukewarm showers. A relief valve spitting water, on the other hand, is a safety device doing its job and telling you something is wrong, and a slow leak at the base can soak into flooring before anyone notices. Smell gas? Leave the house and call your gas utility before anything else. We come prepared to replace elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves, install expansion tanks where pressure is the problem, and make leak repairs at fittings and drain valves.
